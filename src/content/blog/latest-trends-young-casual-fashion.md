## Serve Up Style on and off the Court

Tennis isn't just a sport anymore—it's a whole mood. From crisp polos to bold sneakers, the court-to-street look is everywhere this season. We're seeing young trendsetters mix classic tennis whites with unexpected pops of color and oversized silhouettes.

## The New Tennis Aesthetic

Gone are the days of plain white shorts and basic tees. Today's tennis-inspired fashion is all about mixing sporty with streetwear. Think vintage-inspired racket club jackets, pleated skirts with chunky sneakers, and headbands that double as fashion statements.

### Key Pieces to Watch

- **Vintage polo shirts** in pastels and bold stripes
- **Pleated mini skirts** paired with high-top sneakers
- **Oversized visors** and sweatbands as accessories
- **Retro track jackets** with modern, relaxed fits
- **White sneakers** with bold colored accents

## How Trendsetters Wear It

Alex Rivera, a self-described streetwear enthusiast, tells us: "I love mixing sporty vibes with bold colors. A vintage tennis polo with baggy jeans and my favorite sneakers? That's my go-to."

The beauty of this trend is its versatility. Whether you're actually hitting the court or just hitting the streets, tennis style works for every occasion. Dress it up with a blazer for dinner, or keep it casual with a bucket hat for brunch.

## Make It Your Own

The best part about the tennis trend? There are no rules. Mix high and low, vintage and new, sporty and glam. The key is confidence—wear what makes you feel like a champion, on and off the court.

> "Style is about expressing who you are. Tennis fashion gives you that preppy base, but you can take it anywhere." — Jordan Ellis, Trend Spotter
