Consolidated Gaps (All Sources)

Merged findings from enterprise benchmark, GPT-4o review, and industry frameworks.

Critical

Accessibility Guidelines

Flagged by: Benchmark Frameworks

Every major enterprise system (Mozilla, Material, Fluent, Mailchimp, Atlassian) has dedicated accessibility guidance. We have scattered mentions. Need: WCAG 2.1 AA compliance, contrast ratios, keyboard navigation, screen reader guidance, accessible writing.

Critical

Iconography System

Flagged by: Benchmark

Zero coverage. 6 of 9 benchmarked enterprises treat icons as first-class design elements with grid, stroke weight, sizing, and style rules.

Medium

Customer Service & Crisis Communication

Flagged by: GPT-4o Frameworks

No guidance for support interactions or handling negative press/social crises. Interbrand's "Responsiveness" factor requires this.

Medium

Legal / Copyright / Trademarks

Flagged by: Benchmark Frameworks

No trademark usage rules, copyright notices, or co-branding guidelines. Required for Level 3 maturity and by SpellBrand Enterprise tier.

Medium

Downloadable Asset Package

Flagged by: Benchmark Frameworks

No centralized location for logo files, font files, or brand assets. Mozilla, Uber, and Atlassian all provide this.

Medium

Training & Onboarding

Flagged by: GPT-4o Frameworks

No materials for onboarding new team members. Microsoft Level 4 requires brand training in employee onboarding.

Low

Illustration Style, Component Library, Data Visualization

Flagged by: Benchmark

No illustration guidelines, no documented component patterns (beyond CSS), and no data visualization rules. These are common in design-system-heavy enterprises (IBM, Material, Salesforce) but less critical for a content-focused brand.

Validation Verdict

Enterprise Benchmark: 7.5/10 — Strong on content, voice, photography, and social. Critical gaps in accessibility and iconography.

GPT-4o (Independent): 8.5/10 — "Highly actionable, distinctive, and production-ready." Gaps in customer service, crisis comms, and training.

Maturity: Level 3 of 5 — "Defined" tier. Meets most Level 3 criteria. Needs legal/trademark and training to fully qualify. Level 4 requires analytics and measurement.

Combined assessment: The guidelines are strong for a content-focused brand but not yet fully enterprise-ready. Adding accessibility guidelines and a legal/trademark section would close the two most critical gaps. Adding customer service tone, crisis communication, and iconography would bring the system to a solid Level 4.
