# Brand Identity

## Brand Name

**WKND Trendsetters**

The name signals weekend energy, youth culture, and fashion-forward thinking. "WKND" is informal and abbreviated — like a text from a friend. "Trendsetters" positions the audience as leaders, not followers.

## Tagline

**"Fresh looks, bold moves"**

This is the brand mantra. Use it as a headline, sign-off, or rally cry. It captures both the aesthetic (fresh looks) and the attitude (bold moves).

## Mission

WKND Trendsetters exists to inspire young adults to express themselves through fashion — without gatekeeping, without pretension, and without breaking the bank. We celebrate real people in real moments: courtside, on the dance floor, at the beach, on campus.

## Brand Values

| Value | What It Means |
|---|---|
| **Freshness** | Always current. We move with the culture, never behind it. |
| **Boldness** | Unapologetic about style. No hedging, no "maybe." Own it. |
| **Authenticity** | Real people, real stories. We don't fabricate aspirations. |
| **Inclusivity** | Everyone's welcome. No rules, no dress code for entry. |
| **Fun** | Fashion is play, not homework. If it's not fun, we're doing it wrong. |

## Positioning

**For** young adults (18–30) who see fashion as self-expression, not status.
**We are** a style community that curates trends, tells real stories, and makes fashion feel accessible.
**Unlike** traditional fashion media that feels exclusive or intimidating.
**We** speak like a friend who happens to know what's trending — confident but never condescending.

## Target Audience

### Primary: Young Trendsetters (18–30)

- Active, social, experience-driven
- Fashion = self-expression, not conformity
- Budget-conscious but style-ambitious
- Digital-native: lives on social, shares outfits, follows creators
- Lifestyle scenes: campus, concerts, beach days, parties, city streets, gaming
