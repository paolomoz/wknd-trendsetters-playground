# Social Media Guidelines

## Platform Presence

WKND Trendsetters maintains profiles on five platforms. Each has a distinct role but the same brand voice.

## Per-Platform Guidelines

### Instagram
**Role:** Primary visual platform. The brand's "lookbook."

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Outfit photos, street style, behind-the-scenes, reels of styling tips |
| Tone | Casual, emoji-friendly, caption-light. Let the image lead. |
| Hashtags | 5–10 per post. Mix branded (#WKNDTrendsetters, #FreshLooksBoldMoves) with discovery (#StreetStyle, #OOTD) |
| Stories | Daily: polls, "this or that" style votes, quick tips, reposts from community |
| Reels | Weekly: 15–30s styling transformations, trend breakdowns, "get ready with me" |
| Caption style | Short. 1–2 sentences + emoji. "this fit tho 🔥" over "We are delighted to share..." |

### TikTok (if added)
**Role:** Trend discovery and viral reach.

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Trend reactions, "style in 60 seconds", outfit challenges, duets with community |
| Tone | Most casual of all platforms. Slang welcome. Energy is everything. |
| Posting | 3–5x per week. Quantity matters here. |
| Hook | First 2 seconds must grab attention. Start with the payoff, not the setup. |

### X (Twitter)
**Role:** Real-time trend commentary and conversation.

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Hot takes on trends, quick links to blog posts, community shoutouts, event live-tweeting |
| Tone | Snappy, witty, opinionated. Fragments and one-liners. |
| Thread style | Use threads for trend breakdowns: "a thread on why tennis style is taking over 🧵" |
| Engagement | Reply to mentions. Retweet community content. Be present, not just broadcasting. |

### Facebook
**Role:** Community hub and long-form sharing.

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Blog post shares, event announcements, community discussions, polls |
| Tone | Slightly more complete sentences than X, but still casual. |
| Groups | Consider a "WKND Trendsetters Community" group for deeper engagement |
| Events | Use Facebook Events for any real-world meetups or watch parties |

### YouTube
**Role:** Long-form style content and tutorials.

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Style guides, "day in the life" vlogs, trend deep-dives, hauls, event coverage |
| Tone | Conversational, tutorial-friendly. Like talking to a friend over coffee. |
| Thumbnails | Bold text overlay + expressive face/outfit. Use neon yellow accent. |
| Titles | Follow headline formulas from voice.md. Curiosity-driven. |
| Length | Shorts: 30–60s. Regular: 5–12 minutes. No padding. |

### LinkedIn
**Role:** Industry perspective and brand partnerships.

| Element | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Content | Behind-the-brand stories, trend industry analysis, partnership announcements |
| Tone | Professional but still warm. More "insight" than "vibes." |
| Posting | 1–2x per week. Quality over quantity. |

## Universal Social Rules

### Always
- Respond to comments within 24 hours
- Credit photographers and community members
- Use the brand voice — casual, bold, real
- Include alt text on all images
- Tag relevant people and brands

### Never
- Don't use corporate language on social ("We are pleased to announce...")
- Don't ignore negative comments — address them with warmth
- Don't post without an image or video (text-only posts underperform)
- Don't use more than 3 hashtags on X (looks spammy)
- Don't cross-post identical content to all platforms — adapt per channel

## Branded Hashtags

| Hashtag | Usage |
|---|---|
| `#WKNDTrendsetters` | Primary brand hashtag. Use on all posts. |
| `#FreshLooksBoldMoves` | Campaign/tagline hashtag. Use on hero content. |
| `#WKNDStyle` | Community UGC tag. Encourage followers to use it. |
| `#TrendsetterSpotlight` | For featuring community members. |

## Community Engagement

- Repost/share user-generated content at least 2x per week
- Feature a "Trendsetter of the Week" on Instagram stories
- Run monthly style challenges with a branded hashtag
- Reply with personality, not templates
