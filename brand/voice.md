# Voice & Tone

## Voice Pillars

The WKND Trendsetters voice stays consistent across all channels. These four pillars define how we sound:

### 1. Fresh
Always current, energetic, never stale. We talk about what's happening now, not what happened last season.
- Use present tense
- Reference current moments, not timeless abstractions
- "Fresh" is our most-used word — that's intentional

### 2. Bold
Unapologetic about style choices. We make definitive statements, not wishy-washy suggestions.
- "This is the look" not "this could be a nice option"
- "The golden rule of street style? There are no rules."
- Confidence without arrogance

### 3. Real
Grounded in authentic stories and real people. We don't fabricate lifestyle fantasies.
- Feature real personas with real names
- "See how real people rock new brands"
- Acknowledge real contexts: budget, comfort, practicality

### 4. Playful
Fun-loving, uses personality and humor. Fashion is play.
- "Fashion should be fun, and this is pure fun!"
- Wordplay welcome: "Serve up style on and off the court"
- Energy without being exhausting

## Tone Spectrum

The voice stays the same. The tone shifts by context:

| Context | Tone | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Hero / Headlines** | Maximum energy, punchy | "Fresh fits, bold moves — trends for every vibe" |
| **Blog posts** | Conversational, informative | "Tennis isn't just a sport anymore — it's a whole mood." |
| **Product descriptions** | Enthusiastic, specific | "This oversized tee? Your new going-out-but-make-it-casual essential." |
| **CTAs** | Direct, action-oriented | "See trends" / "Join now" / "Read more" |
| **FAQ / Support** | Friendly, helpful | "We keep it fresh! Check our weekly trend roundups." |
| **Error messages** | Light, reassuring | "Oops — that page went off the grid. Let's get you back on track." |
| **Social media** | Casual, emoji-friendly | "this fit tho 🔥 // new drop just landed" |

## Vocabulary

### Power Words (use often)
Fresh, bold, real, vibes, style, looks, fits, trends, effortless, cool, energy, confidence, fun, serve, own, rock, pop, glow up

### Action Verbs (prefer these)
Rock, serve, own, turn heads, pop, glow up, light up, discover, dive into, join, catch, make your move

### Avoid
- Corporate speak: "leverage," "synergy," "curated experience"
- Fashion gatekeeping: "must-have" (implies judgment), "fashion faux pas"
- Passive voice: "styles were selected" → "we picked these styles"
- Hedging: "might," "perhaps," "could potentially"
- Formal address: "Dear reader," "Ladies and gentlemen"

## Sentence Style

- Short sentences for impact. Long ones for explanation.
- Contractions always: "we've," "they're," "don't" — never "do not"
- Fragments are fine. Great, even.
- Questions engage: "Ready to join the style squad?"
- Exclamation points: use freely but not every sentence

## Headline Formulas

These patterns recur across the site and should be followed for new content:

| Pattern | Example |
|---|---|
| [Adjective] + [noun], [adjective] + [noun] | "Fresh looks, bold stories" |
| [Scene] + [style descriptor] | "Sunkissed and effortless" |
| [Activity] + style twist | "Game on, style up" |
| [Trend] + declaration | "Tennis style, redefined" |
| [Noun] that [verb] | "Party fits that pop" |

## Do / Don't Examples

### Do ✓
- "The sidewalk is your runway"
- "Neon is back in a big way, but this time it's more sophisticated"
- "The real secret to a great party outfit? Wearing something that makes you feel unstoppable"
- "From rooftop parties to late-night hangs, these styles turn heads"

### Don't ✗
- "Our curated selection of athleisure garments represents the pinnacle of contemporary sportswear"
- "Please review our comprehensive style guide for additional sartorial recommendations"
- "Click here to subscribe to our newsletter for periodic fashion updates"
- "We are pleased to announce our latest collection of seasonal offerings"
