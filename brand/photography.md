# Photography & Art Direction

## Image Philosophy

Photography on WKND Trendsetters should feel like a friend's Instagram feed, not a stock photo library. Every image should answer: "Would a real 22-year-old share this?"

## Subject Categories

### Core Subjects (use most often)

| Category | Examples | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Street style** | People in urban settings, sidewalks, city streets | Confident, candid |
| **Social scenes** | Concerts, parties, beach gatherings, campus life | Energetic, communal |
| **Active lifestyle** | Tennis, cycling, dancing, gaming | Dynamic, in-motion |
| **Fashion details** | Outfit close-ups, accessories, shopping moments | Aspirational, tangible |

### Supporting Subjects (use to add variety)

| Category | Examples | Mood |
|---|---|---|
| **Destinations** | Beach, city skylines, adventure spots | Aspirational, wanderlust |
| **Community** | Coffee shops, networking, campus activities | Warm, inclusive |
| **Events** | Concerts, festivals, fundraisers, galas | High-energy, memorable |

## Style Direction

### Do
- **Natural light** preferred — outdoor shots, golden hour, bright daylight
- **Candid over posed** — people mid-action, mid-laugh, mid-conversation
- **Diverse representation** — varied ethnicities, body types, gender expressions, styles
- **Real settings** — streets, beaches, venues, campuses (not studios)
- **Color vibrancy** — rich, saturated colors that feel alive
- **Movement** — capture energy, not stillness
- **Young energy** — subjects should look 18–30 and feel approachable

### Don't
- No heavily filtered or overly processed images
- No studio white-background product shots (this isn't e-commerce)
- No stock photo cliches (handshake, pointing at laptop, fake laughing)
- No images that feel exclusive, luxury, or unattainable
- No dark, moody, high-fashion editorial (too far from the brand energy)

## Technical Specifications

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Format | AVIF (primary), WebP (fallback) |
| Aspect ratios | 3:2 (hero), 1:1 (gallery/cards), 16:9 (wide banners), 4:3 (general) |
| Border radius | `--radius-card` (1.25rem) on all displayed images |
| Loading | `lazy` on all images below the fold |
| Alt text | Required on all images — descriptive, not decorative ("Hip-hop dancer mid-move on city street", not "image1") |

## Image Treatment

- Images on dark overlays use a gradient: `linear-gradient(to top, rgba(0,0,0,0.7) 0%, transparent 60%)`
- Text placed over images must have this overlay for legibility
- Gallery images are displayed with `object-fit: cover` — crops are expected
- No borders on images — rounded corners only

## Image Sourcing Notes

When generating or sourcing new images, prioritize:
1. Lifestyle photography with real-feeling subjects
2. Urban and outdoor settings
3. Fashion-forward but accessible clothing
4. Group shots and social moments (not solo model poses)
5. Action and movement over static poses
